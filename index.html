banana converter

Bananas have a bit of potassium-40 in them, so they’re very slightly radioactive. Some people use that to explain radiation doses: one banana is about 0.1 µSv. I wanted to see how many bananas a chest X-ray is, so I wrote this.

This is a joke with real numbers in it, not a safety tool. Eating a banana doesn’t really give you 0.1 µSv, because your body keeps its potassium level about the same. The doses are rough averages (UNSCEAR and similar). If you’re actually worried about radiation, ask a doctor.
